# Optimal power flow on every MATPOWER case with costs, 2026-11-04

Phase O1's survey: `powerflow`'s DC and AC optimal power flows, solved by `interior_point`'s MIPS,
on the 73 MATPOWER 8.1 cases with generator costs. Each run is compared with MATPOWER's own
results from both its implementations, MP-Core (the default, which the tests compare against)
and the legacy code (which `powerflow` follows). The survey's output, one row per case, is in
[`opf-dc-2026-11-04.csv`](opf-dc-2026-11-04.csv) and [`opf-ac-2026-11-04.csv`](opf-ac-2026-11-04.csv).

## Setup

| | |
|---|---|
| Code | The `dev/opf-survey` branch, at the commit that adds this record |
| Machine and system | As in the earlier records: Intel Core i7-14700K, Ubuntu 24.04.3 under WSL2, Rust 1.97.1, release profile |
| Reference | MATPOWER 8.1 under Octave 8.4.0, run locally by `scripts/generate-reference.sh` (results not committed): `rundcopf` with MIPS forced and `runopf` (MIPS by default), each through MP-Core and through the legacy code (`exp.use_legacy_core`), one case per process with single-threaded BLAS |
| Run | `scripts/benchmark-opf.sh` with `YBUS_BENCH_MODEL` set to `dc`, then `ac`, finishing at 17:39 and 17:59 EST, with the default limits (1 hour and 10 GiB per case) |

`powerflow`'s time is the OPF's setup and solve together, by its median over repeated runs for up
to 1 s or 101 runs (one run for the large cases). MATPOWER's is its own elapsed time (`et`) for
the same, one run per case.

- **Iteration counts match** except on two cases:
  - **DC case2736sp:** 44 iterations to MATPOWER's 45. `powerflow` orders MIPS's KKT systems by

  - **AC case8387pegase:** 82 iterations to 80. Its optimum isn't unique: the reactive dispatch

- **Objectives and bus prices agree,** as in the table. The worst `LAM_P` and `LAM_Q` are on AC

## The dispatch and the bound multipliers aren't unique

The tests compare everything on the committed cases (case14, case30 and case118), whose optima are
unique. Over every case, two things make parts of the solution non-unique, and they're recorded
in the CSVs (`primal_abs`, `multiplier_abs`) but not judged:

- **Degenerate optima.** With linear costs and several generators at the same marginal cost, the
  optimal dispatch is a set, and interior-point methods stop at different points in it.
  - **DC:** on 17 cases the angles, dispatch and flows differ by more than 1e-3, by up to 48.8
    (MW, on case8387pegase), with objectives within 1.5e-14 and `LAM_P` within 4.5e-11.
  - **MATPOWER differs from itself the same way:** MP-Core from legacy by up to 86 MW on
    case8387pegase, with objectives within 1.6e-16.
- **Ill-determined bound multipliers (AC).** Branches of near-zero impedance lock neighbouring
  buses together; when they sit at a voltage limit, the split of the limit's price between their
  multipliers is nearly arbitrary.
  - **On case3012wp:** `mu_vmax` differs by up to 0.43, at neighbouring buses whose differences
    cancel (99% of their magnitude), and `LAM_Q` shifts by 1.16e-4 over the region. The
    objective agrees within 1.3e-12.
  - **MATPOWER's two implementations agree more closely there** (`LAM_Q` 5e-7), since both solve
    the KKT systems through Octave's backslash.

## Octave's least-squares fallback

When a KKT system is singular to machine precision, Octave's backslash warns and returns a
least-squares solution by QR, not the LU solution, and MIPS continues where an exact solve
(MATLAB's, UMFPACK's own, or `powerflow`'s) would stop it on too long a step. The references mark
these runs.

- **DC:** 6 runs (case5, case9target, case16am, case118zh, case136ma and case3120sp). MATPOWER
  fails 5 of them anyway, and so does `powerflow`.
  - **case5:** both converge, at 16 and 17 iterations.
  - **case3120sp:** `powerflow` converges at 148 iterations where MATPOWER fails at 150.
- **AC:** 7 runs, all of which MATPOWER and `powerflow` fail.

Iteration counts aren't compared on these runs.

## MP-Core against legacy

MATPOWER's own implementations agree on the outcome of every run, DC and AC, and on every
converged run's iteration count. Their objectives differ by up to 3.9e-13 (DC) and 6.8e-12 (AC,
outside case8387pegase), against `powerflow`'s 7.5e-12 and 1.1e-11 from MP-Core. On 7 AC runs that fail in both (case145, case1888rte,
case6470rte, case6495rte, case_ACTIVSg10k, case_ACTIVSg25k and case_SyntheticUSA), they fail
after different numbers of iterations (for example 92 and 150 on case_ACTIVSg25k); a failing
run's path is sensitive to rounding. `powerflow` sits as close to MP-Core as legacy does on the
converged runs, except where the multiplier splits above are ill-determined.

## Time against MATPOWER

| Where both converge | DC | AC |
|---|--:|--:|
| Under 1,000 buses | 39 cases: 0.13 s against MATPOWER's 4.5 s | 33 cases: 0.52 s against 5.1 s |
| 1,000 buses or more | 19 cases: 11.9 s against 16.2 s | 13 cases: 61.2 s against 59.5 s |

- **Small cases are dominated by Octave's fixed costs,** so `powerflow` is far faster there:
  over every converged case, 99 times for DC and 22 for AC at the median, up to 1,115 times.
- **On large cases the two are close.** `powerflow` takes 0.64–1.27 times MATPOWER's time for AC
  (the most on case_ACTIVSg2000; 24.7 s against 20.7 s on case8387pegase) and 0.27–1.1 times for
  DC (the most on case_ACTIVSg10k, 4.4 s against 4.0 s).
- **The largest cases fail, and slowly.** case_ACTIVSg25k, case_ACTIVSg70k and
  case_SyntheticUSA fail in MATPOWER and `powerflow` alike (all three DC runs at the iteration
  limit, all AC runs numerically). There `powerflow` takes 1.4–2.9 times MATPOWER's time: 690 s
  against 238 s for case_SyntheticUSA's AC OPF. Each iteration analyzes and factors the KKT
  system afresh; phase O4 targets that.
- **Peak memory** is at most 417 MiB (DC) and 1,097 MiB (AC), on case_SyntheticUSA.
